# Team

Juice is made by [DAIOS Technologies Ltd](https://daios.ai).

The founding team's work follows one trajectory: from bounded rationality,
through bounded strategic interaction, to bounded multi-agent composition. Each
member has independently pursued distributed, bounded architectures as an
alternative to concentrating the capability and control of AI systems.

David Hyland, Nick Bishop and Daniel Jarne Ornia hold a UK AISI Alignment
Project grant to develop a science of bounded multi-agent intelligence at the
University of Oxford, combining multi-agent learning, game theory, mechanism
design and empirical research. Around that agenda they have built an active
AI-safety community through weekly research sessions, seminars and
field-building.
