<h1 class="juice-pitch">Why Juice</h1>

<section class="juice-section" markdown="1">
This page will set out the case for Juice: why the web's next step is capabilities
that humans and agents can call and be paid for, and why that layer belongs to a
network rather than to a platform.

Until it is written, the argument is in the DAIOS vision paper,
[*Web 4.0: A New Paradigm for a Composable, AI-Powered Internet*](https://daios.ai/static/pdf/daios-vision-paper.pdf).
