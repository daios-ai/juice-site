The SVG is preferred by anything modern; the .ico behind it is emitted by the theme from `favicon_ico` in _config.yml, and covers browsers that ignore an SVG icon. {%- endcomment -%} {%- comment -%} The face is self-hosted (see _sass/custom/setup.scss for why). Preloaded because it is declared inside the stylesheet, so the browser would otherwise not learn of it until that stylesheet had parsed. `crossorigin` is required even same-origin: font fetches are made in CORS mode, and without it the preload is discarded and the file fetched a second time. {%- endcomment -%}
