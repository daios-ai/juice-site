# Privacy

This site sets **no cookies** and stores nothing on your device. It shows no
advertising and shares nothing with advertisers.

This site is run by Pedro A. Ortega, <pedro.ortega@gmail.com>.

<h2>Analytics</h2>

Page views and referring sites are counted by Cloudflare Web Analytics. It uses
no cookies, does not fingerprint your browser, and does not recognize you on a
later visit or on another site. Your IP address reaches Cloudflare with the
request, as it does with every request to every website, and is not retained for
analytics. We count visits to know whether the site is read.

<h2>Hosting</h2>

The site is served by GitHub Pages, which processes requests in order to deliver
it. Nothing else on these pages is loaded from anyone else: the typeface, the
icons, the styles and the scripts are all served from this domain.
